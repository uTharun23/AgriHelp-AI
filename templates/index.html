Rice Blast Disease

DISEASED 94.2% Match

Severity: Moderate (24% Leaf Area Affected)

Key Symptoms:

Spindle-shaped spots on leaves, greyish centers with reddish-brown margins.

Chemical Spray Plan:

Apply Tricyclazole 75 WP at 0.6 g/L or Azoxystrobin 25 SC at 1 mL/L.

Organic & Biological Controls:

Foliar spray of Pseudomonas fluorescens at 10g/L or Bacillus subtilis at 5g/L.

Preventive Crop Management:

Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizers, burn/remove crop residues, crop rotation.
